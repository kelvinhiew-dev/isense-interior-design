From first visitto handover从上门量尺到完工交付

A typical wardrobe or kitchen takes about five weeks. Bigger projects take longer in the workshop, but the order stays the same.一般衣柜或厨房项目约需五周。项目越大，工厂制作时间越长，但顺序不变。

  1. Week 1第 1 周

    Site measure上门量尺

    Every wall measured; pipes, sockets and floor level checked.测量每一面墙，检查水管、插座和地面水平。

  2. Weeks 1 to 2第 1 至 2 周

    3D design3D 设计

    Layout, colours and handles, changed until you are happy.确认布局、颜色和把手，改到满意为止。

  3. Week 2第 2 周

    Quotation报价

    Each board, finish and hinge listed with its price.每块板材、饰面和铰链都逐项列价。

  4. Weeks 3 to 4第 3 至 4 周

    Workshop build工厂制作

    Cut, edged and assembled in our workshop, not in your home.开料、封边、组装都在工厂完成，不在您家锯木。

  5. Week 5第 5 周

    Install and handover安装与交付

    One to three days on site. Doors aligned, floor swept.现场一至三天，门板调齐，地面清扫干净。
